After the 27th

Money, the bank and the email

What happens between signing the statutes and receiving the first krone of funding. Written for the board, readable by anyone who wants to know where the 100 kr. goes.

Two things here do happen on 27 September. Photograph every board member's ID before people leave, and send the bank application the same evening. Neither needs a CVR number, and both start a clock that otherwise starts a week later.

The order things happen in

Everything below can start on the day of the founding meeting. The old plan, waiting for the CVR number before talking to a bank, wastes a week for no reason.

  1. Founding meeting, and collect ID

    Signed statutes and signed minutes. From this evening the association exists. Photograph every board member's passport or driving licence before people leave: the bank will ask for all of them, and chasing them afterwards is the single most common delay.

  2. Same day: the email, and the bank application

    Create the association's own email, then send the bank application. A CVR number is not required to apply for a basal erhvervskonto, and Nykredit's form asks whether you have one. Applying now rather than after the CVR starts the legal clock a week earlier.

  3. In parallel: the CVR number

    Register on virk.dk as a frivillig forening, with the chair's personal MitID. Free, a few minutes, receipt the same day and the number published the next. Choose frivillig forening, not almindelig forening: it needs no document uploads. It must be renewed every three years.

  4. The bank answers within 10 working days

    That deadline is the law, not a service promise, as long as the application was complete and the bank is one that the rule binds. Then register the account as NemKonto, which is one line on the bank's form.

  5. Funding

    Private foundations can pay into the account as soon as it exists. Money from Københavns Kommune also needs the folkeoplysende approval and five paying members.

Why not just use someone's own account

It is the first idea every small association has, and the Danish tax authorities treat it as the account holder's money.

Tax

An almennyttig forening is its own tax subject and pays no tax on membership fees or grants. A person does. Money that lands on a personal account can be taxed as that person's income, and it is up to them to prove otherwise. Skattestyrelsen has taken this to court and won: in one published case the people holding power of attorney over an association's accounts were personally assessed on what passed through them.

Continuity

Board members change. If the money is tied to one person's CPR number, every change of treasurer means a new account, and illness or a falling-out locks the association out of its own funds.

Trust

Only the account holder can see a private account. That puts the treasurer in the position of being suspected the moment anything looks off, which is unfair to them.

So: no personal account, not even for a few weeks. The 100 kr. collected on the 27th goes in an envelope, recorded on the member list, and is deposited when the association's account opens. A MobilePay Box pays out to a personal account, so it has the same problem on a smaller scale.

NemKonto, and when it matters

NemKonto is not a separate account. It is a label on an ordinary Danish bank account that tells public authorities where to pay the association. The bank sets it at no cost.

QuestionAnswer
Needed for Any money from a public authority: lokaletilskud and the Udviklingspulje from Københavns Kommune, and state puljer. Public money is paid to NemKonto and nowhere else.
Not needed for Registering the CVR number, collecting membership fees, private foundations like Tuborgfondet, and sponsors. They pay to any account in the association's name.
The catch Only institutions on the nemkonto.dk list can hold one. Wise and Revolut are not on it. Picking one of them now means switching banks later, exactly when a grant is waiting.

Why Nykredit

Cheapest first year of the banks that are actually obliged to say yes, cheapest thereafter, and the only one with an application form you can fill in online instead of booking a branch meeting. Its page states the ten working days explicitly, and its form has a N/A (Forening) option for the audit question, which is a sign associations are expected rather than tolerated.

Merkur is about 800 kr. cheaper in year one and is the better fit on values, but it carries no legal deadline and no right to an account, and its cheap rate is conditional. Worth asking in parallel, not worth waiting for.

The ten-day rule, and what to do if a bank stalls

Since 1 January 2025, lov om betalingskonti § 13 b gives associations a legal right to a basic business account, and § 13 b stk. 5 gives the bank a deadline: it must open the account or refuse within 10 working days of a complete application.

PointWhat it means
Which banks Only banks designated systemically important. For 2026: Danske Bank, Nykredit, Jyske Bank, AL Sydbank, Nordea and Saxo Bank. Merkur, Fælleskassen and Andelskassen are not on the list, so with them there is no deadline and no right.
No CVR needed The law requires an address in Denmark, not a CVR number. The application can go in before the registration comes back.
Refusal is limited Eight grounds, exhaustive, mostly about money-laundering suspicion or refusing to supply ID. "We would rather not take associations" is not one of them.
A refusal must be written Free of charge, on paper or another durable medium, and it must tell you how to appeal.
Where to appeal The bank's own complaints officer first, then Det finansielle ankenævn. In June 2025 it ruled one refusal of a basic business account unlawful, so the route works.

Vi er en frivillig forening med hjemsted i København og ansøger om en basal erhvervskonto efter lov om betalingskonti § 13 b, som også skal fungere som foreningens NemKonto. Vedlagt er vedtægter, referat fra den stiftende generalforsamling samt legitimation på samtlige bestyrelsesmedlemmer. Vi beder om svar inden for fristen på 10 arbejdsdage, jf. § 13 b, stk. 5.

What we are going to do

One application, sent the same evening, to the bank that is both cheapest and obliged to answer.

  1. Collect ID at the meeting

    Passport or driving licence for all three board members, photographed before anyone leaves. This is what banks stall on.

  2. Apply to Nykredit the same evening

    The online form, with the statutes, the minutes and the ID attached, and the paragraph above quoted. A complete application starts the clock; an incomplete one does not.

  3. Ask Merkur in parallel

    Cheaper and a better fit on values, but no deadline and no obligation. If they answer well before Nykredit, take them.

  4. Register the CVR the next morning

    virk.dk, frivillig forening, the chair's MitID. Send the number on to whichever bank is still processing.

  5. Day 11, if it has gone quiet

    Write to the bank citing § 13 b stk. 5 and ask for the written refusal the law requires. That sentence alone usually moves things.

What the bank needs

  • Signed vedtægter and signed minutes
    The minutes must name who was elected, and § 9 shows who may bind the association.
  • !
    ID for every board member, not just the two signatories
    Passport or driving licence. Banks treat the whole board as reelle ejere under the money-laundering rules, and this is the single most common cause of delay. Collect it on the night.
  • A sentence on what the account is for
    Membership fees and grants, a few thousand kroner a year. Banks ask, and a vague answer slows things down.
  • The CVR number, when it arrives
    Not needed to apply. Send it on once virk.dk has published it.

If a grant arrives before the account does: a larger association or a frivilligcenter can receive it for us under a written agreement, with the grant-giver told. It is a bridge, not a plan.

The association's email

§ 5.3 makes email the official way to call a general assembly, and the registration asks for a contact address. It should belong to the association, so it survives a change of chair.

WhenWhat
Now A free Gmail account such as aisundays@gmail.com. Five minutes, no cost, no DNS. The Lithuanian Youth Society runs on exactly this. The password sits with the chair and the treasurer.
Later, optional An address on aisundays.org that forwards to the Gmail. Forwarding is free. Sending as @aisundays.org needs a paid mail service, which is not worth it for a handful of emails a year.
