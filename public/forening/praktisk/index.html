Money, the bank and the email

What happens between signing the statutes and receiving the first krone of funding. Written for the board, readable by anyone who wants to know where the 100 kr. goes.

Researched September 2026 · prices change, confirm with the bank

The order things happen in

Each step needs the one before it. The bank is the slow one, so it gets booked the same week the CVR number arrives.

1

Founding meeting27 September

Signed statutes and signed minutes. From this evening the association exists.

2

Forening email and CVR numberthe week after

Create the association's own email first, because it goes on the registration. Then register on virk.dk as a frivillig forening. Free, takes minutes, done with the formand's personal MitID.

3

Bank account, registered as NemKonto2 to 4 weeks

Book the meeting the week the CVR number arrives. Registering the account as NemKonto is one extra line on the bank's form.

4

Fundingwhen we are ready

Private foundations can pay into the account from step 3. Money from Københavns Kommune also needs the folkeoplysende approval and five paying members.

Why not just use someone's own account

It is the first idea every small association has. The Danish tax authorities treat it as the account holder's money.

Tax
An almennyttig forening is its own tax subject and pays no tax on membership fees or grants. A person does. Money that lands on a personal account can be taxed as that person's income, and it is up to them to prove it was not. Skattestyrelsen has taken this to court and won: in one published case, the people holding power of attorney over an association's accounts were personally assessed on what went through them.
Continuity
Board members change. If the money is tied to one person's CPR number, every change of treasurer means a new account, and illness or a falling-out locks the association out of its own funds.
Trust
Only the account holder can see a private account. That puts the treasurer in the position of being suspected the moment anything looks off, which is unfair to them.

So: no personal account, not even for a few weeks. The 100 kr. collected on the 27th goes in an envelope, recorded on the member list, and is deposited when the association's account opens. A MobilePay Box pays out to a personal account, so it has the same problem on a smaller scale.

NemKonto, and when it matters

NemKonto is not a separate account. It is a label on an ordinary Danish bank account that tells public authorities where to pay the association. The bank sets it at no cost.

Needed for
Any money from a public authority. Lokaletilskud and the Udviklingspulje from Københavns Kommune, and state puljer. Public money is paid to NemKonto and nowhere else.
Not needed for
Registering the CVR number, collecting membership fees, private foundations like Tuborgfondet, and sponsors. They pay to any account in the association's name.
The catch
Only institutions on nemkonto.dk's list can hold one. Wise and Revolut are not on it. Picking one of them now means switching banks later, exactly when a grant is waiting.

The basal erhvervskonto comes from a law in force since 1 January 2025: the largest banks must offer associations a basic account, free or for a "rimeligt gebyr". The law does not define reasonable. The ministry's own figures put the median fee for associations at 600 kr. a year.

What we are going to do

Ask two banks for the basic account by name, and take the cheaper answer.

  1. Email AL Sydbank and Merkur Andelskasse the week the CVR number arrives
  2. Ask for the basal erhvervskonto, registered as NemKonto, and ask for the setup and yearly fee in writing
  3. Anything above 1.500 kr. in the first year is too much for an association turning over a few thousand kroner
  4. Formand and kasserer go to the meeting together, since § 9.1 says the two of them sign jointly

The line to send the bank

What to bring

  • Signed vedtægter and signed minutes from the founding meeting
  • The CVR number
  • Passport or driving licence for everyone who will sign on the account
  • § 9 of the statutes marked, so the bank sees who may bind the association

If a grant arrives before the account does: a larger association or a frivilligcenter can receive it for us under a written agreement, with the grant-giver told. It is a bridge, not a plan.

The association's email

§ 5.3 makes email the official way to call a general assembly, and the registration asks for a contact address. It should belong to the association, so it survives a change of formand.

Now
A free Gmail account such as aisundays@gmail.com. Five minutes, no cost, no DNS. The Lithuanian Youth Society runs on exactly this. The password sits with the formand and the kasserer.
Later, optional
An address on aisundays.org that forwards to the Gmail. Forwarding is free. Sending as @aisundays.org needs a paid mail service, which is not worth it for a handful of emails a year.
