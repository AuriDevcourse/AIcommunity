After the 27th

Money, the bank and the email

What happens between signing the statutes and receiving the first krone of funding. Written for the board, readable by anyone who wants to know where the 100 kr. goes.

Two things here do happen on 27 September. Photograph every board member's ID before people leave, and send the Nykredit backup application that evening. Everything else waits for the CVR number, which is filed the next morning.

The order things happen in

Everything below can start on the day of the founding meeting. The old plan, waiting for the CVR number before talking to a bank, wastes a week for no reason.

  1. Founding meeting, and collect ID

    Signed statutes and signed minutes. From this evening the association exists. Photograph every board member's passport or driving licence before people leave: the bank will ask for all of them, and chasing them afterwards is the single most common delay.

  2. Same day: the email, and the bank application

    Create the association's own email, then send the bank application. A CVR number is not required to apply for a basal erhvervskonto, and Nykredit's form asks whether you have one. Applying now rather than after the CVR starts the legal clock a week earlier.

  3. In parallel: the CVR number

    Register on virk.dk as a frivillig forening, with the chair's personal MitID. Free, a few minutes, receipt the same day and the number published the next. Choose frivillig forening, not almindelig forening: it needs no document uploads. It must be renewed every three years.

  4. The bank answers within 10 working days

    That deadline is the law, not a service promise, as long as the application was complete and the bank is one that the rule binds. Then register the account as NemKonto, which is one line on the bank's form.

  5. Funding

    Private foundations can pay into the account as soon as it exists. Money from Københavns Kommune also needs the folkeoplysende approval and five paying members.

Why not just use someone's own account

It is the first idea every small association has, and the Danish tax authorities treat it as the account holder's money.

Tax

An almennyttig forening is its own tax subject and pays no tax on membership fees or grants. A person does. Money that lands on a personal account can be taxed as that person's income, and it is up to them to prove otherwise. Skattestyrelsen has taken this to court and won: in one published case the people holding power of attorney over an association's accounts were personally assessed on what passed through them.

Continuity

Board members change. If the money is tied to one person's CPR number, every change of treasurer means a new account, and illness or a falling-out locks the association out of its own funds.

Trust

Only the account holder can see a private account. That puts the treasurer in the position of being suspected the moment anything looks off, which is unfair to them.

So: no personal account, not even for a few weeks. The 100 kr. collected on the 27th goes in an envelope, recorded on the member list, and is deposited when the association's account opens. A MobilePay Box pays out to a personal account, so it has the same problem on a smaller scale.

NemKonto, and when it matters

NemKonto is not a separate account. It is a label on an ordinary Danish bank account that tells public authorities where to pay the association. The bank sets it at no cost.

QuestionAnswer
Needed for Any money from a public authority: lokaletilskud and the Udviklingspulje from Københavns Kommune, and state puljer. Public money is paid to NemKonto and nowhere else.
Not needed for Registering the CVR number, collecting membership fees, private foundations like Tuborgfondet, and sponsors. They pay to any account in the association's name.
The catch Only institutions on the nemkonto.dk list can hold one. Wise and Revolut are not on it. Picking one of them now means switching banks later, exactly when a grant is waiting.

Why Sparekassen Danmark

600 kr. a year, nothing to set up. Their Foreningsservice price list shows opening at 0 kr., changes at 0 kr., running account fee at 0 kr., and transfers in netbank at 0 kr. The only obligatory charge is netbank including insurance at 150 kr. a quarter. Board portal Unioo, normally 129 kr. a month, is included free. A payment card is 200 kr. a year if you want one, and the association does not need one to start.

The usual objection to a sparekasse is that it only serves its own corner of Jutland. Not this one. They have five branches in Copenhagen, the first opened in 2006, and they are the eighth largest bank in Denmark. They are also on the NemKonto list, so public grants can be paid in.

The one thing they are not is legally obliged to say yes. They are not a SIFI bank, so the ten working days and the right to an account do not apply. That is what the parallel application is for.

The backup, in the same week

Apply to Nykredit at the same time. It costs 4.500 kr. in year one, which is too much, but it is legally obliged to answer within ten working days and cannot refuse except on eight narrow grounds. If Sparekassen Danmark says yes, withdraw the Nykredit application and lose nothing.

Never leave the founding without a bank on the way. One free application and one obliged application, sent the same evening, covers both failure modes.

The ten-day rule, and what to do if a bank stalls

Since 1 January 2025, lov om betalingskonti § 13 b gives associations a legal right to a basic business account, and § 13 b stk. 5 gives the bank a deadline: it must open the account or refuse within 10 working days of a complete application.

PointWhat it means
Which banks Only banks designated systemically important. For 2026: Danske Bank, Nykredit, Jyske Bank, AL Sydbank, Nordea and Saxo Bank. Merkur, Fælleskassen and Andelskassen are not on the list, so with them there is no deadline and no right.
No CVR needed For the statutory basal erhvervskonto the law requires an address in Denmark, not a CVR number, and Nykredit's form asks whether you have one. This is a right against SIFI banks only. Sparekassen Danmark is not one, and its own page says a CVR number is required, so the cheap route does wait for virk.dk.
Refusal is limited Eight grounds, exhaustive, mostly about money-laundering suspicion or refusing to supply ID. "We would rather not take associations" is not one of them.
A refusal must be written Free of charge, on paper or another durable medium, and it must tell you how to appeal.
Where to appeal The bank's own complaints officer first, then Det finansielle ankenævn. In June 2025 it ruled one refusal of a basic business account unlawful, so the route works.

Vi er en frivillig forening med hjemsted i København og ansøger om en basal erhvervskonto efter lov om betalingskonti § 13 b, som også skal fungere som foreningens NemKonto. Vedlagt er vedtægter, referat fra den stiftende generalforsamling samt legitimation på samtlige bestyrelsesmedlemmer. Vi beder om svar inden for fristen på 10 arbejdsdage, jf. § 13 b, stk. 5.

What we are going to do, day by day

Sparekassen Danmark requires a CVR number before it will open an account, so the order is fixed: found, register, then bank. Nykredit does not require one, which is why it can go out the same evening as the backup.

  1. Sunday 27 September, at the meeting

    Sign the statutes and the minutes. Photograph passport or driving licence for all three board members before anyone leaves, because getting everyone in a room again is the hard part. Collect the 100 kr. in cash against the member list.

  2. Sunday evening, the backup application

    Apply to Nykredit online. Its form asks whether you have a CVR number and accepts no, so this one does not wait. Free to apply and withdrawn the moment the sparekasse says yes.

  3. Monday 28 September, the CVR

    virk.dk, Start virksomhed, tab Foreninger, institutioner og fonde, then Frivillig forening. Free, any MitID will do, it does not have to be a board member. Receipt by email the same day; the number is published in CVR the next morning.

  4. Tuesday 29 September, the bank

    With the CVR number in hand, contact a Copenhagen branch. Their own page says to become a customer through a branch, not through Foreningsservice, so walk into Gammel Strand 42 or call the branch first.

  5. Around 13 October, if Nykredit has gone quiet

    Ten working days from a complete application. Cite § 13 b stk. 5 and ask for the written refusal the law requires.

The CVR registration, screen by screen

Monday morning, 15 minutes, free. Use a laptop: virk.dk says outright that this form is not built for small screens. One thing to do first, and it is worth the two minutes: search datacvr.virk.dk for "AI Sundays" and confirm nobody holds the name already.

  1. Open the form and log in

    virk.dk → Start frivillig forening, then Start selvbetjening. Auri's personal MitID. The association has no MitID of its own yet, which is the bit that confuses people. Any MitID works, it does not have to be a signatory.

  2. Pick the type

    Tab Foreninger, institutioner og fonde, then Frivillig forening. Frivillig, not almindelig: it means no reporting obligation to the authorities, and it is the right box for us.

  3. Fill the association's details

    Name exactly as § 1.1 writes it, Foreningen AI Sundays. Start date is 27 September, the founding date, not the day you file. Address, Copenhagen municipality, the association's email and a phone number. Branchekode is required and has no "none" option; 94.99.00 Andre organisationer og foreninger i.a.n. is the safe pick and can be changed later.

  4. Purpose, board, signing rule

    Paste § 2.1 for the purpose and § 9.1 for the signing rule, both word for word. The board is the three members with their CPR numbers. Andrei is auditor and does not go in the board field. A mismatch between CVR and the statutes is what sends you back for a second meeting.

  5. Add a foreningsrepræsentant

    Optional, do it anyway. It attaches one person who can then act for the association with their private MitID, including reading its Digital Post. virk.dk's own text says most associations can skip administering MitID Erhverv entirely this way. Reversible; the name stays listed in CVR afterwards.

  6. Upload and submit

    Signed statutes and signed minutes, as PDFs rather than photos. Receipt by email the same day. The number is public on datacvr.virk.dk the next morning, not instantly, so the bank visit is Tuesday.

Two things about the date

virk.dk has announced maintenance on 28 September, 16:30–17:30 and 18:30–19:30. File in the morning and neither matters. And download the registreringsbevis when it arrives: that certificate is what you send anyone who asks whether AI Sundays is a real organisation.

Set a reminder for September 2029

A frivillig forening CVR number expires after three years unless renewed, and the only warning is an email to the address on the filing. If that address dies, the CVR number dies with it. Renewal is free. This is the main reason to use a shared association address rather than anyone's personal one.

If a bank quotes you a big fee anyway

Two levers, both real, both worth one email before accepting a number.

LeverHow it works
Ask to be graded low-risk Finanstilsynet's money-laundering guidance on associations lets a bank classify a forening as lavrisiko when it has a small economy, democratic structure and open accounts. That cuts the bank's own compliance work, which is what it is charging for. Ask in writing to be assessed against it.
Make them justify the fee For the basal erhvervskonto the law says free or a rimeligt gebyr. The explanatory notes define reasonable as the bank's actual costs plus a reasonable profit, and the industry asked for a market-comparison test instead and was refused. So "this is what everyone charges" is not a defence. Ask the bank to justify the figure on its own costs.
If it still will not move Det finansielle ankenævn has run a dedicated basal-erhvervskonto complaints track since January 2025. It costs 1.000 kr., refunded if you win. No published decision has yet tested a fee level, so this would be a first. Worth knowing, not worth doing for a 600 kr. account.

What the bank needs

  • Signed vedtægter and signed minutes
    The minutes must name who was elected, and § 9 shows who may bind the association.
  • !
    ID for every board member, not just the two signatories
    Passport or driving licence. Banks treat the whole board as reelle ejere under the money-laundering rules, and this is the single most common cause of delay. Collect it on the night.
  • A sentence on what the account is for
    Membership fees and grants, a few thousand kroner a year. Banks ask, and a vague answer slows things down.
  • The CVR number, when it arrives
    Not needed to apply. Send it on once virk.dk has published it.

If a grant arrives before the account does: a larger association or a frivilligcenter can receive it for us under a written agreement, with the grant-giver told. It is a bridge, not a plan.

The association's email

§ 5.3 makes email the official way to call a general assembly, and the registration asks for a contact address. It should belong to the association, so it survives a change of chair.

WhenWhat
Now A free Gmail account such as aisundays@gmail.com. Five minutes, no cost, no DNS. The Lithuanian Youth Society runs on exactly this. The password sits with the chair and the treasurer.
Later, optional An address on aisundays.org that forwards to the Gmail. Forwarding is free. Sending as @aisundays.org needs a paid mail service, which is not worth it for a handful of emails a year.
