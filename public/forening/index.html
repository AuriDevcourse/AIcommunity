What it changes

Three things, and the first happens the evening we sign.

The community owns itself

A legal name, an address and a bank account that belong to AI Sundays rather than to Auri personally. Under § 9.4 no member and no board member is personally liable for anything the association does.

Rent covered, 65 to 75%

Lokaletilskud from the municipality. Matrikel1 stays home for now, but this is what makes a bigger room possible when we need one.

Grants become possible

Tuborgfondet answers in about a week, average grant 30.000 kr. Nobody is applying for anything yet.

§ 1Navn og hjemsted

1.1Foreningens navn er Foreningen AI Sundays. I daglig tale, i kommunikation og på foreningens materialer anvendes den korte form AI Sundays.

1.2Foreningen er hjemmehørende i Københavns Kommune.

§ 2Formål

2.1Foreningens formål er at drive folkeoplysende virksomhed, der styrker deltagernes praktiske forståelse af kunstig intelligens og digitale værktøjer gennem regelmæssig, fælles læring.

2.2Formålet søges opnået ved:

  1. a)At afholde tilbagevendende åbne værksteder, hvor deltagerne lærer at bruge kunstig intelligens til at bygge og færdiggøre egne projekter. Værkstederne afholdes som udgangspunkt hver anden søndag og mindst 12 gange årligt.
  2. b)At understøtte deltagernes faglige udvikling, portefølje og beskæftigelsesmuligheder gennem kollegial vejledning og deling af viden.
  3. c)At samarbejde med uddannelsesinstitutioner, biblioteker, foreninger, virksomheder og offentlige institutioner om oplysning om kunstig intelligens.
  4. d)At formidle viden om ansvarlig og kritisk brug af kunstig intelligens.

2.3Foreningen er almennyttig, non-profit, uafhængig af partipolitiske og religiøse interesser og drives af frivillige. Et eventuelt overskud anvendes udelukkende til foreningens formål og kan ikke udbetales til medlemmerne.

§ 3Medlemskreds

3.1Foreningen er åben for alle, som tilslutter sig foreningens formål og betaler kontingent. Der stilles ingen krav om nationalitet, bopælsland, uddannelse, faglig baggrund eller forudgående kendskab til kunstig intelligens.

3.2Medlemskab opnås ved indmeldelse og betaling af kontingent. Bestyrelsen fører en medlemsfortegnelse med navn, adresse, fødselsdato og kontaktoplysninger. Medlemsfortegnelsen behandles fortroligt og i overensstemmelse med databeskyttelseslovgivningen.

3.3Medlemskab gælder for det regnskabsår, kontingentet dækker.

3.4Udmeldelse sker ved skriftlig meddelelse til bestyrelsen og har virkning fra udgangen af den periode, kontingentet dækker. Betalt kontingent refunderes ikke.

3.5Et medlem, der ikke betaler kontingent inden 30 dage efter påkrav, betragtes som udmeldt uden yderligere varsel.

3.6Bestyrelsen kan ekskludere et medlem, der handler til væsentlig skade for foreningen eller groft tilsidesætter vedtægterne. Eksklusionen kan af medlemmet kræves prøvet på den førstkommende generalforsamling, som træffer endelig afgørelse ved simpelt flertal.

§ 4Kontingent

4.1Kontingentet fastsættes af generalforsamlingen for et år ad gangen.

4.2Kontingentet ved foreningens stiftelse er 100 DKK pr. år.

4.3Bestyrelsen kan i enkelttilfælde bevilge hel eller delvis kontingentfritagelse af sociale hensyn. Kontingentfritagelse påvirker ikke medlemmets rettigheder.

§ 5Generalforsamling

5.1Generalforsamlingen er foreningens højeste myndighed.

5.2Ordinær generalforsamling afholdes hvert år inden udgangen af april.

5.3Indkaldelse sker skriftligt, herunder ved e-mail, med mindst 14 dages varsel og med angivelse af dagsorden.

5.4Dagsordenen for den ordinære generalforsamling skal mindst indeholde:

  1. Valg af dirigent og referent
  2. Bestyrelsens beretning om det forløbne år
  3. Fremlæggelse og godkendelse af det reviderede årsregnskab
  4. Fremlæggelse af budget og fastsættelse af kontingent
  5. Behandling af indkomne forslag
  6. Valg af bestyrelse
  7. Valg af revisor
  8. Eventuelt

5.5Forslag fra medlemmer skal være bestyrelsen i hænde senest 7 dage før generalforsamlingen.

5.6Alle medlemmer, der har betalt kontingent, har møde-, tale- og stemmeret. Hvert medlem har én stemme. Der kan ikke stemmes ved fuldmagt.

5.7Generalforsamlingen er beslutningsdygtig uanset antallet af fremmødte medlemmer. Beslutninger træffes ved simpelt flertal blandt de fremmødte, medmindre vedtægterne kræver kvalificeret flertal. Ved stemmelighed bortfalder forslaget.

5.8Der afholdes skriftlig afstemning, hvis blot ét medlem begærer det.

5.9Der udarbejdes referat, som underskrives af dirigenten og formanden.

§ 6Ekstraordinær generalforsamling

6.1Ekstraordinær generalforsamling afholdes, når bestyrelsen finder det nødvendigt, eller når mindst 1/3 af medlemmerne skriftligt kræver det med angivelse af de emner, der ønskes behandlet.

6.2Den ekstraordinære generalforsamling indkaldes med mindst 14 dages varsel og afholdes senest 6 uger efter, at kravet er modtaget.

§ 7Bestyrelsen

7.1Foreningen ledes mellem generalforsamlingerne af en bestyrelse på 3 til 7 medlemmer, valgt af generalforsamlingen for ét år ad gangen. Genvalg er muligt.

7.2Bestyrelsen konstituerer sig selv med formand, kasserer og eventuelt næstformand og sekretær.

7.3Bestyrelsen er beslutningsdygtig, når mindst halvdelen af medlemmerne er til stede. Beslutninger træffes ved simpelt flertal. Ved stemmelighed er formandens stemme afgørende.

7.4Bestyrelsen afholder møde mindst fire gange årligt og fører referat over sine beslutninger.

7.5Bestyrelsen varetager foreningens daglige ledelse, fastlægger aktiviteterne, søger tilskud og fondsmidler, forvalter foreningens midler i overensstemmelse med formålet og fører foreningens arkiv.

7.6Bestyrelsen kan nedsætte arbejdsgrupper og inddrage frivillige, som ikke er bestyrelsesmedlemmer.

7.7Bestyrelsesarbejdet er ubetalt. Dokumenterede udgifter afholdt på foreningens vegne refunderes efter bestyrelsens godkendelse.

7.8Udtræder et bestyrelsesmedlem i utide, kan bestyrelsen supplere sig selv indtil næste generalforsamling, forudsat at bestyrelsen fortsat består af mindst 3 medlemmer.

§ 8Økonomi, regnskab og revision

8.1Foreningens regnskabsår er kalenderåret. Første regnskabsår løber fra stiftelsen til den 31. december samme år.

8.2Foreningens indtægter kan bestå af kontingenter, offentlige tilskud, fondsmidler, sponsorater, donationer, deltagerbetaling ved enkelte arrangementer og andre lovlige indtægter.

8.3Kassereren fører foreningens regnskab og forvalter foreningens midler. Foreningens midler indsættes på en konto i foreningens navn. Kontant beholdning holdes på et minimum.

8.4Årsregnskabet revideres af den revisor, generalforsamlingen har valgt, og fremlægges til godkendelse på den ordinære generalforsamling.

8.5Generalforsamlingen vælger for ét år ad gangen én revisor, som ikke må være medlem af bestyrelsen. Generalforsamlingen kan i stedet vælge en registreret eller statsautoriseret revisor, hvis et tilskud kræver det.

§ 9Tegningsret og hæftelse

9.1Foreningen tegnes af formanden i forening med kassereren, eller af formanden i forening med to øvrige bestyrelsesmedlemmer.

9.2Ved køb, salg eller pantsætning af fast ejendom samt ved optagelse af lån tegnes foreningen af den samlede bestyrelse.

9.3Bestyrelsen kan meddele kassereren fuldmagt til at disponere over foreningens konti i det daglige inden for et af bestyrelsen fastsat beløb.

9.4Foreningen hæfter alene med sin formue. Hverken medlemmer eller bestyrelsesmedlemmer hæfter personligt for foreningens forpligtelser.

§ 10Vedtægtsændringer

10.1Vedtægtsændringer kræver, at mindst 2/3 af de fremmødte stemmeberettigede medlemmer på en generalforsamling stemmer for.

10.2Forslag til vedtægtsændringer skal fremgå af indkaldelsen og udsendes til medlemmerne sammen med denne.

10.3Ændringer træder i kraft ved generalforsamlingens vedtagelse, medmindre andet besluttes.

§ 11Opløsning

11.1Foreningen kan opløses, når mindst 2/3 af de fremmødte stemmeberettigede medlemmer stemmer for på to generalforsamlinger afholdt med mindst 14 dages og højst 6 ugers mellemrum.

11.2Ved opløsning anvendes foreningens eventuelle formue til almennyttige, folkeoplysende formål i Københavns Kommune efter den opløsende generalforsamlings beslutning. Formuen kan under ingen omstændigheder udbetales til medlemmerne.

11.3Eventuelle uforbrugte offentlige tilskud tilbagebetales til tilskudsgiver.

§ 1Name and registered office

1.1The name of the association is Foreningen AI Sundays. In everyday use, in communications and on the association's materials, the short form AI Sundays is used.

1.2The association is domiciled in Københavns Kommune.

§ 2Purpose

2.1The purpose of the association is to carry out non-formal public education activities (folkeoplysning) that strengthen the participants' practical understanding of artificial intelligence and digital tools through regular, collaborative learning.

2.2The purpose is pursued by:

  1. a)Holding recurring open workshops where participants learn to use artificial intelligence to build and finish their own projects. Workshops are normally held every second Sunday and at least 12 times a year.
  2. b)Supporting participants' professional development, portfolio and employment prospects through peer guidance and knowledge sharing.
  3. c)Collaborating with educational institutions, libraries, associations, companies and public institutions on public education about artificial intelligence.
  4. d)Communicating knowledge about responsible and critical use of artificial intelligence.

2.3The association is charitable, non-profit, independent of party-political and religious interests, and run by volunteers. Any surplus shall be used exclusively to further the association's purpose and may not be distributed to the members.

§ 3Membership

3.1The association is open to all who support the association's purpose and pay the membership fee. No requirements are imposed regarding nationality, country of residence, education, professional background or prior knowledge of artificial intelligence.

3.2Membership is obtained by enrolment and payment of the membership fee. The board maintains a membership register containing name, address, date of birth and contact details. The membership register is treated confidentially and in accordance with data protection legislation.

3.3Membership is valid for the financial year covered by the fee.

3.4Withdrawal is effected by written notice to the board and takes effect at the end of the period covered by the fee. Fees already paid are not refunded.

3.5A member who fails to pay the membership fee within 30 days of a payment demand is deemed to have withdrawn without further notice.

3.6The board may expel a member who acts to the material detriment of the association or grossly disregards the articles of association. The expelled member may demand that the expulsion be reviewed at the next general assembly, which shall make a final decision by simple majority.

§ 4Membership fee

4.1The membership fee is determined by the general assembly for one year at a time.

4.2The membership fee at the founding of the association is DKK 100 per year.

4.3The board may in individual cases grant full or partial exemption from the membership fee on social grounds. An exemption from the fee does not affect the member's rights.

§ 5General assembly

5.1The general assembly is the association's supreme authority.

5.2An ordinary general assembly is held every year no later than the end of April.

5.3Notice is given in writing, including by e-mail, with at least 14 days' notice and stating the agenda.

5.4The agenda for the ordinary general assembly shall include at least the following:

  1. Election of a chair and a minute-taker
  2. The board's report on the past year
  3. Presentation and approval of the audited annual accounts
  4. Presentation of the budget and setting of the membership fee
  5. Consideration of proposals received
  6. Election of the board
  7. Election of the auditor
  8. Any other business

5.5Proposals from members must reach the board no later than 7 days before the general assembly.

5.6All members who have paid the membership fee have the right to attend, speak and vote. Each member has one vote. Proxy voting is not permitted.

5.7The general assembly has a quorum regardless of the number of members present. Decisions are made by simple majority of those present, unless the articles of association require a qualified majority. In the event of a tie, the proposal is defeated.

5.8A written ballot shall be held if so requested by even a single member.

5.9Minutes shall be drawn up and signed by the chairperson of the meeting and the chairperson of the board.

§ 6Extraordinary general assembly

6.1An extraordinary general assembly shall be held when the board deems it necessary, or when at least 1/3 of the members request it in writing, stating the matters to be considered.

6.2The extraordinary general assembly shall be convened with at least 14 days' notice and held no later than 6 weeks after the request has been received.

§ 7The board

7.1Between general assemblies the association is managed by a board of 3 to 7 members, elected by the general assembly for one year at a time. Re-election is possible.

7.2The board constitutes itself with a chairperson, a treasurer and, optionally, a vice-chairperson and a secretary.

7.3The board has a quorum when at least half of its members are present. Decisions are made by simple majority. In the event of a tie, the chairperson's vote is decisive.

7.4The board shall meet at least four times per year and keep minutes of its decisions.

7.5The board is responsible for the day-to-day management of the association, determines its activities, applies for grants and funding, manages the association's funds in accordance with the purpose and maintains the association's archives.

7.6The board may establish working groups and involve volunteers who are not board members.

7.7Board work is unpaid. Documented expenses incurred on behalf of the association are reimbursed upon approval by the board.

7.8If a board member resigns before the end of their term, the board may co-opt a replacement until the next general assembly, provided the board continues to consist of at least 3 members.

§ 8Finances, accounts and audit

8.1The association's financial year is the calendar year. The first financial year runs from the date of founding to 31 December of the same year.

8.2The association's income may consist of membership fees, public grants, foundation funding, sponsorships, donations, participation fees for individual events and other lawful income.

8.3The treasurer keeps the association's accounts and manages its funds. The association's funds shall be deposited in an account in the association's name. Cash holdings shall be kept to a minimum.

8.4The annual accounts are audited by the auditor elected by the general assembly and presented for approval at the ordinary general assembly.

8.5The general assembly elects for one year at a time one auditor, who may not be a member of the board. The general assembly may instead elect a registered or state-authorised auditor, if required by a grant condition.

§ 9Power to bind the association and liability

9.1The association is bound by the joint signatures of the chairperson together with the treasurer, or by the chairperson together with two other board members.

9.2For the purchase, sale or mortgaging of real property and for the taking out of loans, the association is bound by the full board acting jointly.

9.3The board may grant the treasurer a power of attorney to operate the association's accounts in the day-to-day course of business within a limit set by the board.

9.4The association is liable only to the extent of its assets. Neither members nor board members are personally liable for the obligations of the association.

§ 10Amendments to the articles of association

10.1Amendments to the articles of association require that at least 2/3 of the members present who are entitled to vote at a general assembly vote in favour.

10.2Proposed amendments to the articles of association must be included in the notice of meeting and distributed to the members together with said notice.

10.3Amendments take effect upon adoption by the general assembly, unless otherwise decided.

§ 11Dissolution

11.1The association may be dissolved when at least 2/3 of the members present who are entitled to vote do so at two general assemblies held with an interval of at least 14 days and no more than 6 weeks.

11.2Upon dissolution, any remaining assets of the association shall be applied to charitable, public-education purposes in Københavns Kommune as decided by the dissolving general assembly. Under no circumstances may the assets be distributed to the members.

11.3Any unspent public grants shall be returned to the granting authority.
