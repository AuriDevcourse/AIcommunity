# AI Workshop #8 — 2026-06-14

**Title:** AI tools across business & creativity
**Location:** TBD
**Attendees:** Auri, Andrei, Sany, Eividas, Ignas

## About This Session
The session covered various applications of AI tools across different domains such as image generation, business trading, creative projects, project management, design and development, cryptocurrency trading, and gaming. The group discussed specific tools like Mato elements, Adobe Illustrator's AI features, VAE, Blender, and others, focusing on their practical uses and potential benefits

## Topics
### AI Tools for Image Generation
The discussion centered around various AI tools used in image generation. Auri highlighted the use of Mato elements, Adobe Illustrator's AI features, and VAE for specific sections of websites. Andrei mentioned the potential of AI in creating 3D models and generating SVG files from vector forms

### AI in Business and Trading
Participants explored the use of AI in business purposes such as trading platforms and data labeling services. Ignas shared his experience with AI tools like Claude for drawing tasks, emphasizing potential risks associated with small budget trades due to tax issues and increased scrutiny from accountants. He also recommended Koinly for simplifying crypto platform report exports

### AI in Creative Projects
The group discussed various creative applications of AI, including generating brand guides, creating 3D models with Blender and Fusion, and using AI to enhance presentations. Auri described a presentation tool he built for saving containers as PDFs, while Ignas discussed his efforts to integrate pictures into specific projects without overriding manual changes

### AI in Project Management
The conversation delved into project management tools with a focus on security and API limitations. Eividas suggested using 'AS' for the project name due to its ease of searchability, while Andrei highlighted the need for better UX design in presentations. The group also touched upon the use of CLAUDE.md for security workflows and design files

### AI in Design and Development
The discussion included various AI tools used in design and development, such as OpenClaw, Hermes, and Stingy. Andrei mentioned downloading models from Ollama via API URL and the potential of AI to create 3D models for technical purposes. Sany proposed using AI to generate photographs for better fitting visuals

### AI in Cryptocurrency Trading
The group debated the use of AI in cryptocurrency trading, with Ignas warning about the risks and potential losses associated with small budget trades. He recommended connecting history with trading platforms to avoid increased scrutiny from accountants but noted that accounting costs are high despite providing legal advice

### AI in Gaming
The discussion included AI applications in gaming, such as recreating GTA Six and using AI for data labeling. Ignas shared his experience with a platform called CrowdSomething where people can label data for free but get paid small amounts. Auri mentioned the use of AI to create brand guides by copying images from new websites
